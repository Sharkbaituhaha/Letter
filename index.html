My Hope,
I’ve been thinking about this for a while, and I wanted to say it in a way that comes from my heart. Before anything else, thank you. Thank you for letting me know the real you the happy you, the makulit you, the overthinking you, and even the version of you that gets scared to trust people. Every time you open up to me, I don’t see someone who’s difficult to love. I see someone with a genuinely kind heart who has been trying so hard despite everything she’s been through. That’s what made me fall for you. I love how kind you are, even when you don’t realize it. I love how you care about people, how you appreciate the little things, and how you make me smile with the simplest conversations. Kahit yung random stories mo o yung mga “nonsense” mong kwento, I never get tired of listening because they’re yours. I like you for who you are not for a perfect version of you, but for the real you. And yes, I love you. Not because of one moment, but because every day I find another reason to choose you. I don’t want to rush us, and I don’t want my feelings to stay as words that are only implied. I want to be intentional. So this is me asking you properly… Pwede ba kitang ligawan? I want to earn your trust, your smile, and your love the right way. I want my consistency to speak louder than promises, and I want to be someone who brings you peace instead of doubt. I know there are no perfect people, but if you’ll let me, I’ll do my best to love you with honesty, patience, and respect. No matter what your answer is, I’m grateful that I met someone as wonderful as you. Always,
Yours sweet baby,
Frank
